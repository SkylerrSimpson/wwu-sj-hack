# Hardware limitations (not yet implemented)

### Boats without AIS

Most boats under 65 ft don't have to carry AIS, so we can't see them.

- **Fix:** coastal radar matched against AIS.
- **Limits:** misses small, low boats; affected by weather and sea state; can't identify anyone.

### AIS switched off or faked

- **Fix:** satellite radar (Global Fishing Watch).
- **Limits:** misses most boats under 15 m and anything within 1 km of shore.

### Patchy reception

- **Fix:** a Lummi-owned shore AIS receiver.
- **Limits:** improves reception only; doesn't help with boats that have no AIS.

### Identifying the operator

- **Fix:** shore and thermal cameras.
- **Limits:** short range; the Nation has to decide on privacy; a person still reviews the footage.
